## Monthly check

1. Compare public prices and actual available offers; distinguish legacy purchase listings from new-user offers.
2. Check recent app releases and playback, timer, offline and favourite changes.
3. Sample recent positive and negative reviews; note sample size and avoid generalizing prevalence.
4. Confirm whether reported defects still exist before positioning against them.
5. Update the baseline and assess implications for the curated, one-tap bedtime audio concept.
